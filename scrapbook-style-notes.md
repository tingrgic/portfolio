# Scrapbook direction

The seven supplied images were inspected for this revision. Two notebook references establish curved cream pages, many thin edges and a rounded black cover lip. Three mixed-media references establish marker strokes beyond photocopied cut edges, layered silhouettes and strong outline accents. The two Tom examples establish coarse print/paper texture, not a character to reproduce. Watermarked reference imagery is not shipped.

Implementation: original generated notebook material under selectable handwritten text; real project screenshots, cream cutout borders, two diagonal tapes, cyan or rust marker backing, halftone and fiber overlays. Each mobile project gets its own page.

Use: warm scanned-paper feeling; clipped website printouts; faded tape; uneven paper edges; small graphite captions; hand-drawn circles and margin marks; subtle halftone patches. Keep large quiet margins. Limit collage rotation to a few degrees and keep reading text level.

The left work page is Komon's cream and burgundy printout; the right is PK Normal's dark, high-contrast visual. A vermilion “selected” mark ties them together. The images carry the texture; readable captions remain outside image crops. Mobile stacks the two compositions as actual notebook pages instead of compressing a desktop spread.

Avoid cutout people without a reason, novelty type, thick fake drop shadows, torn edges on every item, and decorative clutter over links.
